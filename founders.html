Vrikshā Saār/Founders

Two architects. One long conversation.

Vrikshā Saār was founded in 2022 by Aakash Khurana and Jill Gajera — a practice built on the belief that a building should be argued into existence before it is drawn.

Co-Founder

Aakash Khurana

Principal Architect · Urban Designer · Planner

Aakash works across the whole range the practice is named for — from the way a room meets a courtyard to the way a housing block meets a street. He co-founded Vrikshā Saār in 2022 and also founded Banyan Fig.

His side of the studio sits closest to the planning questions: orientation, wind, water, density, and the public life that happens between buildings — the decisions taken long before anything is visible on site.

Co-Founder

Jill Gajera

Architect

Jill co-founded Vrikshā Saār in 2022. Her work runs the length of a project — from the first conversation with a client through to the last detail settled on site.

Material, light, proportion and joinery are her territory: the interiors and the three-inch decisions that a drawing never fully resolves, and that only get made by standing in the room.

How it works between them

“Architecture doesn’t always rely on bigger gestures. Sometimes it’s a six-inch pool of water that cools an entire house.”

Two people who agree on very little except the thing that matters — that the argument has to happen first, out loud, with the client in the room.

Beyond the two

The rest ofthe collective

Vrikshā Saār runs as a collective rather than a hierarchy. Everyone draws, everyone visits site, and everyone is expected to defend a position out loud.
