01 — The Name

वृक्ष Vrikshā the tree — what is rooted, what keeps growing
सार Saār the essence — what is left once the noise is drawn out

We are a collective of architects and spatial researchers who believe a building is an argument before it is an object.

Vrikshā Saār was founded in 2022 and works across Delhi, Rajkot and Sonepat. We take on residences, housing, interiors and urban questions — and we take each one apart in conversation first: with the client, with the site, with the climate it has to survive.

The result is rarely the loudest idea in the room. It is usually the one that keeps doing quiet work long after everybody has stopped looking at it.

“Architecture doesn’t always rely on bigger gestures. Sometimes it’s a six-inch pool of water that cools an entire house.”

02 — Architecture & Urban Design

Where thework lives

  • 01

    Architecture

    Ground-up buildings where climate, ritual and the site write the brief long before style gets a vote.

  • 02

    Interior Architecture

    Rooms built from material, light and proportion — then argued over for an hour because the artwork sits three inches off.

  • 03

    Urbanism & Housing

    Housing as a living system rather than a stack of units — green courts, shaded spill-outs, terraces that behave like verandahs.

  • 04

    Spatial Research

    The discourse that precedes the drawing. Reading a place, its patterns and its people until a position emerges.

  • 05

    Landscape & Water

    Courts, pools and planting that do climatic work — evaporative cooling, shade, and the small daily rituals that follow them.

  • 06

    Making & Site

    A drawing is a proposal. Site is the argument. We stay on it until the two agree.

04 — How It Goes

Four rooms,in order

  1. I

    Discourse

    We sit down and disagree productively. Client, site, budget, climate and ritual each get a turn before anyone opens a drawing.

  2. II

    Enquiry

    Spatial research — precedent, orientation, wind, water, and the way the street already behaves. The position is written here.

  3. III

    Drawing

    Plan, section and model, iterated until the idea survives contact with structure, services and cost.

  4. IV

    Making

    On site with the mason, the carpenter and the fabricator, resolving the last three inches in person.
